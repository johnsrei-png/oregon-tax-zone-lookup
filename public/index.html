Oregon tax district lookup

Enter an Oregon street address to see its transit district, county, and whether it falls inside the Metro tax boundary or the Portland/Multnomah business tax area.

Upload an .xlsx or .csv file, pick the address columns, and download the results as Excel.

Results come from public boundary data (U.S. Census, Oregon Metro RLIS, and transit district boundary files). They're not an official tax determination. For filings, confirm with the Oregon Department of Revenue and the City of Portland Revenue Division.
